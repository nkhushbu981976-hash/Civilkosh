Standards basis
IS 1786:2008 covers high-strength deformed steel bars and wires for concrete reinforcement and lists nominal bar sizes and strength grades. Nepal's NBC 205:2024 identifies IS 456:2000 and SP 34:1987 among its related standards for low-rise RC work. Use the project-specific NBC/IS seismic and detailing provisions where applicable.
Useful conversion: bar area = πφ²/4. For estimating steel mass from diameter, nominal mass ≈ φ²/162 kg/m when φ is in millimetres.
Reference warning. Bar size, area and mass do not by themselves establish required reinforcement, lap length, anchorage, spacing, ductility or cover. Those depend on the member design, exposure, seismic detailing and governing project documents.
