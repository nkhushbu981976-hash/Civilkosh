Engineering basis

Nepal's DUDBC publishes NBC 110: Plain and Reinforced Concrete and NBC 205:2024 for specified low-rise RC buildings. NBC 205:2024 identifies NBC 105:2020, IS 875 Parts I/II, IS 456:2000 and SP 34:1987 as related standards. These CivilKosh tools state their narrower calculation scope explicitly and do not claim to replace complete structural design.
