STRUCTURAL • PRELIMINARY CHECK

Column Load Check

Check a rectangular tied RCC column for factored axial load capacity, reinforcement percentage, short-column slenderness and basic tie detailing. The axial-capacity equation is deliberately limited to the short, axially loaded case.

Design Inputs

Use factored axial load at the column section. This is not a substitute for a P–M interaction or seismic frame design.

Use the effective length from the structural analysis model, not simply the storey height unless justified.

Calculation Basis

Calculation Basis: Ac = Ag − Asc. Axial capacity Pu = 0.4 fck Ac + 0.67 fy Asc. The load comparison uses the entered factored axial load directly against this ultimate capacity. The minimum eccentricity shown is max(L/500 + least lateral dimension/30, 20 mm).

Code & Assumptions

IS 456:2000 — Cl. 39.3 and 26.5.3
The Nepal RC framework identifies NBC 110 as Plain and Reinforced Concrete; published Nepal engineering guidance also identifies IS 456:2000 as the referenced RC design basis. NBC 205:2024 is a separate ready-to-use guideline for specified low-rise buildings and is not substituted for a full engineered column design here.
  • Rectangular, tied, short column only: slenderness must be ≤ 12 in each relevant direction; this page screens using the least lateral dimension.
  • Longitudinal steel is checked against 0.8%–6% of gross area and at least four bars; bar diameter is checked at ≥12 mm.
  • Tie diameter is checked as max(6 mm, one-fourth of largest longitudinal bar); tie pitch is limited to the least of least dimension, 16 times smallest longitudinal bar diameter and 300 mm.
  • The capacity equation is for axial compression and does not design for actual bending moments, biaxial interaction, second-order effects, seismic ductile detailing or foundation effects.

Engineering Warning

Engineering warning. Most building columns are subjected to moment as well as axial load. Do not use a PASS here as approval for construction. A structural engineer must perform the complete load combination, P–M interaction, slenderness, seismic and detailing checks required for the project.
