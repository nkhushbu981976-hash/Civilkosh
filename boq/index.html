ENGINEERING BASIS

Measurement & Rate Notes

Quantity calculation

Worksheet-supported dimensional quantities use the transparent product of entered terms: L × W × H/D × Number × Factor. Blank dimensional terms are omitted; manual quantity remains available when the project measurement method differs.

Rate basis

Rates are project-specific user inputs. CivilKosh does not insert or imply a government rate. Official rate-analysis norms are reference data only and must be checked for the applicable version, item and project specification.

Nepal reference

For relevant road and bridge work, consult the Department of Roads Standard Specifications for Road and Bridge Works, 2073 (3rd Amendment 2082) and applicable DoR rate-analysis norms, including published amendments. Building work may require applicable DUDBC/NBC provisions and project specifications.

Trust boundary

Code/specification requirements, measurement practice, rate-analysis references and project-specific inputs are different layers. The contract BOQ, drawings, specifications and approved rates control the project.

Engineering / commercial warningVerify drawings, site measurements, specifications, contract BOQ, approved rates and applicable measurement/payment clauses before using an estimate for procurement, certification or payment. CivilKosh does not determine contractual measurement or payment rules.
