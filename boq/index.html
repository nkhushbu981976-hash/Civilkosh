ENGINEERING BASIS

Measurement & Rate Notes

Quantity calculation

For worksheet-supported dimensional items, the transparent calculation is L × W × H/D × Number × Factor. Manual quantity remains available when the project measurement method is different.

Rate basis

Rates are project-specific user inputs. CivilKosh does not insert or imply a government rate. Where official rate-analysis norms are consulted, the applicable source, version and project specification must be checked separately.

Nepal reference

DoR Standard Specifications and DoR Norms for Rate Analysis are reference frameworks for relevant road/bridge work; building work may require applicable DUDBC/NBC provisions and project specifications.

Trust boundary

This workspace organizes quantities and costs; it does not determine whether a measurement method or rate is contractually applicable to a particular project.

Engineering / commercial warningVerify drawings, site measurements, specifications, contract BOQ, approved rates and applicable measurement/payment clauses before using an estimate for procurement, certification or payment.
