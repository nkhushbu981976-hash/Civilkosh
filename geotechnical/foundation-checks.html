Calculation basis: required area = P / qa; service bearing pressure = P / (B × L). A preliminary PASS means the calculated average service pressure does not exceed the entered allowable bearing pressure.

Calculation Basis

This tool is intentionally limited to a vertical, concentric service-load pressure screen for an isolated footing. It does not derive the allowable soil pressure; that value should come from the geotechnical investigation/design basis.

  • No eccentricity, moment, wind/seismic load, sliding, overturning or uplift is included.
  • Footing self-weight, soil overburden, groundwater effects and load-combination factors are not added automatically; enter a load and allowable pressure consistent with the project design basis.
  • Concrete grade is retained as a project input but is not used to claim a structural footing capacity.

Code & Assumptions

For Nepal low-rise RC guidance, NBC 205:2024 states that foundation design is designed using IS 456:2000 and detailed using SP 34 with NBC 105. Its foundation section provides recommended configurations for specified low-rise buildings. This calculator does not reproduce those full structural foundation provisions.

Where allowable bearing pressure is derived analytically, shallow-foundation bearing capacity is commonly evaluated using IS 6403:1981 together with the site-specific geotechnical investigation. Settlement must be checked independently.

Engineering Limitations

Preliminary check only. A PASS is only a soil-pressure screen. Final isolated-footing design requires structural flexure, one-way and punching shear, development/anchorage, minimum reinforcement, column-footing load transfer, settlement, eccentricity, seismic and construction/detailing checks by a qualified engineer.
