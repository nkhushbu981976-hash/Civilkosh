Code & Assumptions

Method: preliminary rectangular shallow-footing bearing-capacity calculation using the bearing-capacity factors and shape/depth-factor framework of IS 6403:1981. Nepal's building-code framework is published by DUDBC; NBC 205:2024 addresses specified low-rise RC buildings and its foundation design references IS 456:2000, while this calculator is a narrower geotechnical check.

  • Vertical, centrally applied load; no inclination, eccentricity, seismic reduction, slope correction or layered-soil correction is included.
  • Effective-stress parameters c′ and φ′ are expected for the drained calculation. Enter measured/project values where available.
  • B is the smaller footing dimension and L is the larger dimension.
  • The displayed groundwater adjustment is intentionally simplified; the full IS 6403 water-table procedure should be used for detailed design.

Engineering Limitations

Preliminary check only. Bearing capacity does not by itself establish foundation adequacy. Settlement, eccentricity, sliding, overturning, seismic effects, groundwater variation, layered soils, slope stability and structural footing design must be checked separately by a qualified geotechnical/structural engineer.
