Method & assumptions

  • Manning uniform-flow equation is used: Q = (1/n)AR^(2/3)S^(1/2), with R = A/P.
  • Section is assumed prismatic, steady and approximately uniform; entrance, backwater, local losses and sediment effects are not modelled.
  • For trapezoidal sections, side slope z is horizontal:vertical.
  • Velocity comments are screening guidance only; no universal Nepal pass/fail velocity limit is imposed.

Nepal engineering basis & limitations

Nepal Department of Roads guidance on side drains describes steady uniform-flow drainage calculations using Manning's equation and the same hydraulic-radius relationship. DoR also publishes current Standard Specifications for Road and Bridge Works. This tool is a preliminary hydraulic calculation, not a substitute for project-specific hydrology, freeboard, erosion, hydraulic-structure, sediment or safety checks.

Sources: Department of Roads drainage guidance and Standard Specifications for Road and Bridge Works. Verify the current project specification and design brief before construction.
