Standards & engineering basis

For Nepal building plumbing/rainwater provisions, consult NBC 208: Sanitary and Plumbing Design Requirements from the Department of Urban Development and Building Construction. For road drainage, consult the current Department of Roads Standard Specifications for Road and Bridge Works and applicable DoR drainage guidance. The two checks on this page use the Rational Method, Q = CiA, and Manning's equation for a rectangular drain; these are preliminary engineering methods and do not establish the project's required rainfall, return period, freeboard, erosion protection or hydrologic model.

Limitations

Rainfall-runoff estimation is highly site and catchment dependent. This workspace does not perform IDF analysis, time-of-concentration determination, watershed routing, inlet-control analysis, culvert design, hydraulic grade-line modelling or detailed flood-risk assessment. Verify all design inputs against the current project survey, hydrology and governing specifications.
