Method & assumptions

  • Darcy–Weisbach: hf = f(L/D)V²/(2g), with g = 9.81 m/s².
  • Laminar friction uses f = 64/Re. Turbulent friction uses the explicit Swamee–Jain approximation.
  • The pipe is treated as flowing full with constant circular diameter and steady conditions.
  • Only straight-pipe friction is calculated. Minor losses, fittings, entrance/exit losses, pump efficiency and surge are not included.

Engineering limitations

Use project-specific pipe material roughness and fluid properties. Transitional flow should be treated cautiously. The optional head field is only added to the reported friction loss; it is not a full hydraulic-gradient or system-energy analysis.
